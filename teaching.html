Undergraduate Mentoring

University of Virginia, 2023–present. I have mentored 12 UVA undergraduates on projects in software engineering, database design, web development, digital accessibility, and mathematics writing.

  • A student-built prototype mapping tool led to a software engineering internship and then a full-time position in backend development upon graduation.
  • A student-implemented static site generator now serves as the production platform hosting the xTensiv Library. This led to a research article in math education co-authored with the student.
  • Current students (CS/Math dual majors) are contributing database architecture, custom Obsidian plugin development, and accessibility features.
